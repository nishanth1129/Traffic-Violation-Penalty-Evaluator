Methodology

How we arrive at the numbers behind every estimate.

1

Catalogue baseline

We surveyed published penalty schedules across multiple US jurisdictions and selected representative midpoints for each violation.

2

Aggravator modeling

Common modifiers (school zone, work zone, repeat offense, accident, minor passenger) apply transparent multipliers and additive points.

3

Tiered offenses

Speeding and DUI use tiered logic that reflects how most states actually escalate penalties (e.g. BAC ≥ 0.15 triggers aggravated DUI).

4

Risk percentages

Jail and suspension percentages are calibrated estimates - they reflect typical likelihood, not certainty.

Important disclaimer

PenaltyEval is an educational tool. Estimates are based on common US-style penalty structures and do not represent any specific jurisdiction's legal code.

Actual fines, points, license consequences, and jail time depend on your state, your county, the citing officer, the judge, your prior record, and many other factors we cannot model.

This is not legal advice. For any citation that could affect your license, livelihood, or freedom, consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction before making decisions.
