Resources

Useful links and reading.

Curated official sources and helpful tools - all external, all free.

Official sources

DMV / DOT lookup

Search "[your state] DMV traffic violations" for the official penalty schedule and points table.

NHTSA

National Highway Traffic Safety Administration - federal data on crashes, recalls, and safety campaigns.

State court websites

Most counties let you pay fines, request hearings, and view ticket records online.

Approved traffic schools

DMV websites publish lists of approved courses for point reduction.

Knowing your rights

ACLU - Know Your Rights

Detailed guides on what you can and can't be required to do during a stop.

FindLaw / Avvo

Free directories of traffic attorneys, often with first-consultation offers.

Insurance Information Institute

Independent data on how violations affect insurance rates by state.

State Bar Association

Lawyer referral and (often) free legal aid programs for qualifying drivers.

Glossary

Demerit point
A unit added to your driving record for moving violations. Accumulating too many triggers license actions.
Citation
The formal name for a traffic ticket. May be civil (fine + points) or criminal (court appearance required).
Implied consent
By accepting a driver's license, you agree to chemical testing if suspected of DUI. Refusal carries automatic penalties.
Adjudication
The court process of deciding guilt or innocence on a citation.
Habitual offender
A driver with multiple major violations within a short window - typically triggers harsher penalties and longer suspensions.
Wobbler
An offense that can be charged as either a misdemeanor or felony depending on circumstances (common with reckless driving).
